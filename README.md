# 2B-or-not-to-be
“You are what you repeatedly do. Excellence, then, is not an act, but a habit.”
~Aristotle

2B is a desktop app built on Python (using the tkinter package). Its intended purpose is self-motivation throughout the day, to help the user build habits for success and productivity. After all, you are what you do everyday!

# Principle

The idea behind 2B is that every time you decide whether to do something productive or to procrastinate, you are choosing between two buttons to press – a Winner button and a Loser button. Each time you perform a good action (e.g., complete a task, wake up early, exercise etc.) you press the Winner button. Whereas, each time you perform a negative action (e.g., eat junk food, sleep in, cut corners on a task or postpone meetings etc.) you press the Loser button. At the end of the day, you just want the Loser button count to not be higher than the Winner button count, in order to avoid being a ‘Loser’.

But it is you who decide what qualifies as a winner/loser action. This is where self-motivation comes in. Your Winner/Loser score is for you to keep track of how productive your day has been. You also don’t always need to be a Winner. We all need a break sometimes and that is okay. You just need to keep the big picture in mind.

# Using the App

In the Decision field, enter the action you performed.
Press the Winner/Loser button as you see fit. This will update the Winner/Loser count and the corresponding action list below the buttons.
If you wish to reconsider a decision entry, you can also select the entry in the list and hit ‘delete’ to remove it.
At the end of the day, go to Menu and click on Finish Day to complete the log.
You do not need to keep the app running at all times. You may close the app, and then load data from the Menu to restore the previously entered data. However, you can only load the last save before the app was closed and nothing prior to that.
Remember, this app is to help you not feel like a loser even when the to-do list seems never-ending. Celebrate the small victories! Build habits that will make you successful in the long run, and not just one particular endeavour.
